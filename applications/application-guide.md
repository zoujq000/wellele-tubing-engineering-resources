# Industrial Tubing Application Guide

WELLELE Tubing provides polymer tubing and insulation solutions for industrial OEM applications.

The correct tubing selection depends on electrical requirements, temperature conditions, mechanical environment and assembly requirements.

## Electrical Insulation Applications

Polymer tubing is widely used for:

- Wire protection
- Electrical component insulation
- Motor insulation
- Transformer insulation

Common materials:

- Mylar tube
- Polyimide tube
- Fiberglass tubing
- Nomex tube

## Motor and Power Electronics

High performance insulation tubing helps protect components in:

- Motors
- EV power systems
- Electrical assemblies
- Power electronics

Polyimide and high temperature materials are often selected for demanding environments.

## Fastener and Bolt Protection

Mylar tubing can be used as insulation sleeves for:

- Bolts
- Fasteners
- Metal components

Applications include electrical isolation and surface protection.

## Sensors and Precision Equipment

Thin-wall polymer tubing can provide:

- Component protection
- Electrical insulation
- Dimensional consistency

Applications include:

- Sensors
- Laboratory equipment
- Precision instruments

## Material Selection

Engineers should evaluate:

- Temperature range
- Electrical performance
- Mechanical requirements
- Chemical exposure
- Dimensional tolerance
