# Industrial Tubing Material Comparison Guide

Selecting the right tubing material depends on temperature, electrical requirements, mechanical performance and application environment.

## Mylar Tube vs Polyimide Tube

| Feature | Mylar Tube | Polyimide Tube |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Polyester Film | Polyimide |
| Temperature Performance | General industrial applications | High temperature applications |
| Electrical Insulation | Good | Excellent |
| Flexibility | Good | Good |
| Cost Level | Economical | Higher performance |
| Typical Use | Component protection, insulation sleeves | High temperature electrical insulation |

## Mylar Tube

Suitable for:

- Electrical insulation
- Fastener insulation sleeves
- Component protection
- General industrial applications

Advantages:

- Lightweight
- Stable dimensions
- Cost effective

## Polyimide Tube

Suitable for:

- High temperature environments
- Precision electrical insulation
- Semiconductor equipment
- Advanced industrial applications

Advantages:

- Excellent thermal stability
- High dielectric performance
- Reliable dimensional control

## How Engineers Select Tubing Materials

Important selection factors:

- Operating temperature
- Voltage requirements
- Mechanical stress
- Chemical environment
- Required service life
