 # WELLELE Tubing Engineering Resources

Technical resources for engineers, OEM manufacturers and industrial buyers working with polymer tubing and electrical insulation materials.

## Product & Material Topics

### Mylar Tube

Engineering information covering Mylar tube, Mylar tubing, polyester film tubing, heat-shrink Mylar tube and non-heat-shrink Mylar tube.

Typical topics include:

* Electrical insulation
* Fastener and bolt insulation sleeves
* Motor and transformer applications
* Dimensional selection
* OEM customization

[Mylar Tube Engineering Resources](https://welleletubing.com/products/mylar-tube/)

### Polyimide Tube

Technical information covering polyimide tube, PI tube and high-temperature insulation tubing.

Manufacturing processes include:

* Extruded polyimide tubing
* Dip-coated polyimide tubing
* Flat-wound polyimide tubing
* Spiral-wound polyimide tubing

Typical applications include electrical insulation, sensors, precision equipment, motors and industrial electronics.

[Polyimide Tubing Resources](https://welleletubing.com)

### Nomex Tube

Engineering information about aramid-paper insulation tubing, including spiral-wound Nomex tube structures for electrical and thermal insulation applications.

### PEEK Tube

Technical resources covering high-performance PEEK tubing for industrial applications requiring temperature resistance, mechanical performance and chemical resistance.

### Fluoropolymer Tubing

Engineering information covering PTFE, FEP, PFA and related fluoropolymer tubing solutions.
