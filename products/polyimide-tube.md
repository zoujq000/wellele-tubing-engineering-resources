# Polyimide Tube Engineering Guide

## Overview

Polyimide tube is a high performance insulation tubing solution for applications requiring thermal stability and electrical insulation.

## Manufacturing Processes

Main processes include:

- Extruded polyimide tubing
- Dip coated polyimide tubing
- Flat wound PI tubing
- Spiral wound PI tubing

## Applications

Typical applications:

- Electrical insulation
- Sensors
- Semiconductor equipment
- Industrial electronics

## Selection Factors

Engineers evaluate:

- Temperature resistance
- Dielectric performance
- Dimensional tolerance
- Mechanical flexibility
