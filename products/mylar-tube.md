# Mylar Tube Engineering Guide

## Overview

Mylar tube is a polyester film based tubing solution used for electrical insulation, component protection and industrial applications.

## Material Characteristics

Key characteristics include:

- Electrical insulation performance
- Lightweight structure
- Dimensional stability
- Moisture resistance

## Applications

Common applications:

- Electrical components
- Bolt and fastener insulation sleeves
- Transformer components
- Motor insulation

## Selection Factors

Engineers usually consider:

- Inner diameter
- Outer diameter
- Wall thickness
- Temperature requirements
- Installation method
